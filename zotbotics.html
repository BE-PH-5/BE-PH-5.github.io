6-DOF Arctos Robotic Arm — ZOTBotics
Goal & Motivation
The requirement was to assemble and wire a fully functional 6-DOF open-source Arctos robotic arm, then design a custom end effector capable of completing three distinct competition tasks: weight lifting, cup stacking, and a freestyle bartender routine involving pouring water between cups.
How I Fulfilled the Requirements
Handled the full electrical assembly and wiring of the arm, integrating stepper motor actuators, limit switch sensors, and an Arduino MEGA for computer-controlled motion. Designed a custom claw attachment from scratch, engineering the grip geometry and actuation to handle both rigid objects and a pouring motion requiring controlled tipping force. Delivered a formal project presentation covering all design decisions and competition results.
Results & Lessons Learned
The arm completed all three competition tasks. The main lesson was in mechanical-electrical integration: the claw design had to account for how the arm's positioning accuracy translated to real-world grip reliability. Small alignment errors in the wiring harness created backlash that showed up in the end effector.
