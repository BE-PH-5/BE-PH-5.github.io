About Me

Engineer by habit,
lab rat by choice.

Computer Engineering sophomore at UC Irvine focused on embedded systems, hardware design, and making things actually work in the real world.

I'm Benjamin Hoang, a Computer Engineering sophomore at UC Irvine (B.S., expected June 2028). My focus is on embedded systems, power distribution, and hardware design — the side of engineering where decisions you make on a schematic show up as real voltage drops and heat in the real world.

I got my first real exposure to engineering at Raytheon Technologies in 2022, where I designed a water distribution monitor in Fusion 360 and wrote embedded C logic to automate flow shutoff. That combination of physical design and firmware has stayed my core interest ever since.

Right now I'm building a remote-controlled rover with a custom PCB and C++ firmware through UCI IEEE, while deepening my circuits fundamentals through EECS coursework. I deliberately limit myself to one major project commitment at a time — I'd rather go deep than spread thin.

Outside engineering I'm drawn to the unglamorous work: staying late to debug a circuit, running a test one more time, documenting what failed so the next build is cleaner. The resume lines follow from that, not the other way around.
